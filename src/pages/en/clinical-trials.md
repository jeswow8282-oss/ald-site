> **Before reading**
> If you are considering participation, **discuss it with your treating clinicians first.** Every trial has strict eligibility criteria — age, phenotype, disease stage, prior treatment — and the summaries here cannot tell you whether you qualify.

## No trial has a site in Korea

<!-- 갱신블록 -->

Of the 74 ALD-related studies registered on ClinicalTrials.gov, **not one has a study site in Korea.**

This is more than an inconvenience. It means Korean patients will reach new treatments **last**. For a sponsor to consider Korea as a trial location, there has to be **data on how many patients there are and what condition they are in** — and at present that does not exist.

## Therapeutic trials

### Leriglitazone in adult cerebral ALD (CALYX)

A **phase 3** trial of the efficacy and safety of leriglitazone in adult men with cerebral disease. In paediatric cerebral ALD the drug has reached a positive regulatory opinion in Europe; this trial addresses adults.

`ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING` — running, not currently enrolling
Sponsor: Minoryx Therapeutics · Started July 2023
→ [NCT05819866](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05819866)

### Dimethyl fumarate in adrenomyeloneuropathy

A **phase 2/3** trial in AMN. Dimethyl fumarate, used in multiple sclerosis, targets oxidative stress and neuroinflammation.

`ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING`
Principal investigator: Aurora Pujol (Spain) · Started April 2024
→ [NCT06513533](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06513533)

### Treating leg symptoms in women with X-linked ALD

A **phase 4** trial addressing leg symptoms in women with ALD — an unusual study in that it is a therapeutic trial in female patients only.

`ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING`
Massachusetts General Hospital · Started April 2023
→ [NCT05003648](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05003648)

### Intrathecal and intravenous lentiviral gene therapy

Lentiviral gene therapy delivered both intrathecally and intravenously.

`RECRUITING`
Shenzhen Geno-Immune Medical Institute (China) · Started August 2025
→ [NCT03727555](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03727555)

## Transplantation trials

### Reduced intensity conditioning for non-malignant disorders

A **phase 2** trial of reduced-intensity conditioning in umbilical cord blood, bone marrow and peripheral blood stem cell transplantation, including ALD among non-malignant indications.

`RECRUITING` · Paul Szabolcs (University of Pittsburgh) · Started 2014
→ [NCT01962415](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01962415)

### Cord blood transplant with intrathecal cord blood–derived oligodendrocyte-like cells

A **phase 1** trial in inherited metabolic diseases, combining cord blood transplantation with intrathecal administration of cord blood–derived cells.

`RECRUITING` · Joanne Kurtzberg (Duke University) · Started 2014
→ [NCT02254863](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02254863)

### Allogeneic HCT for metabolic disorders and severe osteopetrosis (MT2013-31)

`ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING` · University of Minnesota · Started 2014
→ [NCT02171104](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02171104)

## What the status labels mean

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RECRUITING` | Currently enrolling participants |
| `NOT_YET_RECRUITING` | Registered but enrolment has not opened |
| `ENROLLING_BY_INVITATION` | Enrolling selected participants only |
| `ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING` | Running, but enrolment has closed |
